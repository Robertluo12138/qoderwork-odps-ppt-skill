# PPT Template Catalog

This file tracks the current first-round template system for `odps-ppt-report-builder`.

## Visual Direction

- White background first
- Brand color `#FF6200` (90%) and accent `#FFA020` (10%) per VI spec
- Orange brand emphasis instead of heavy color blocking
- Light surfaces (`#FFF4EB`) and weak borders (`#FFDCC8`)
- More whitespace, less dashboard density
- Suitable for management review rather than raw BI export
- Logo placed on cover (top-left) and every page footer (bottom-left)
- See `references/brand_vi_spec.md` for full color and logo rules

## First-Round Templates

### `cover`

- Purpose: open the report with a clear business and period signal
- Feeling: formal, brand-led, calm
- Typical slide type: `title`

### `overview`

- Purpose: explain the overall business condition first
- Feeling: management summary, not side-by-side comparison
- Typical slide type: `table`

### `kpi_summary`

- Purpose: show the 3 to 6 most important numbers at a glance
- Feeling: clean metric cards with one-line management takeaway
- Typical slide type: `table`

### `comparison`

- Purpose: support the story with trend or comparison evidence
- Feeling: analysis aid, not the opening hero page
- Typical slide type: `chart`

### `conclusion`

- Purpose: leave leadership with a short set of clear conclusions
- Feeling: calm, structured, not dense
- Typical slide type: `ai_bullets`

### `thanks`

- Purpose: close the deck without a cheap template feel
- Feeling: minimal, clean, brand-consistent
- Typical slide type: `ai_bullets`
